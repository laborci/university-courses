# Presentation outline

## Main claim

In one or two sentences, state what you want the audience to understand.

## Outline

1. An opening question or a short concrete example.
2. An explanation of the key concept.
3. A real service or case.
4. A takeaway: why does this matter to a computing professional?

## Sources

## Checklist before uploading

- `slides_url` is a publicly viewable Google Slides link.
- The topic clearly matches one of the available topics.
- The outline is brief but shows the presentation’s line of argument.
- Sources are listed in the file.
- The file is located exactly at `presentation/presentation.md` in your public repository.
