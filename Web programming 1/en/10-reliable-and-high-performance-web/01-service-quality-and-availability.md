# Service Quality and Availability

**Key message:** A good web service is not merely fast when everything is normal; it remains useful, predictable, and recoverable when components or networks fail.

Availability describes the proportion of time a service can perform its intended function, but it should be defined from the user’s perspective. A page may respond while payment, login, or submission is unavailable. Reliability also includes correctness, data integrity, recoverability, and clear expectations. Redundancy, backups, tested recovery, sensible dependencies, and realistic service objectives support these properties.

## Glossary

**Availability:** ability to provide an intended service when needed.  
**Reliability:** consistent correct behaviour over time.  
**Service objective:** a measurable target for service behaviour.
