# Homework 10 – Analysing a traffic peak

Create one `homework/10.md` file about a fictional traffic-peak scenario. Choose one service type: ticket sales, course registration, appointment booking, or online exam registration.

## Task

1. Name the selected service and one critical user task.
2. Explain why a sudden traffic peak might occur.
3. Identify two public resources that can be cached effectively and two personal or fresh data items that cannot simply be cached.
4. Name three observations or metrics that would help detect the problem.
5. Describe three possible actions: one about user communication, one about limiting or distributing load, and one about temporarily restricting less important functions.
6. Explain one trade-off between performance, security, cost, or freshness.

The purpose is to understand the situation, not to create an operational runbook or a concrete implementation.
