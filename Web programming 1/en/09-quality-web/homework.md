# Homework 9 – Evaluating the quality of a web page

Create one `homework/09.md` file evaluating a freely chosen, publicly available web page.

## Task

1. Give the URL and the page’s main purpose.
2. Evaluate accessibility using at least three observations. Consider heading hierarchy, keyboard operation, focus, contrast, or alternative text for images.
3. Evaluate responsiveness and usability in two different screen-size situations, observed or reasoned about.
4. Describe what may help or hinder discoverability, such as title, description, semantic structure, understandable content, or links.
5. Identify two data-protection or tracking questions raised by using the page.
6. Give three concrete, prioritized improvement suggestions and explain the expected user impact of each.

Do not give legal advice; the data-protection part is about recognizing web-related considerations.
