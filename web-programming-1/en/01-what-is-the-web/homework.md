# Homework 1 – Mapping a web service

Create one `homework/01.md` file about a freely chosen web service that you use regularly.

## Task

1. Name the service and summarize its purpose in two or three sentences.
2. Give one URL from the service and break it down into scheme, domain name, path, and—if present—parameters and fragment.
3. Identify at least five actors or components involved in its operation, such as the user, browser, content provider, server, search engine, CDN, or payment provider.
4. Describe the relationship between these actors in short, arrow-separated steps.
5. Explain in five to eight sentences why this is a web service and why browser access is beneficial.

Do not use personal or confidential data. The file must be understandable on its own, without attachments.
